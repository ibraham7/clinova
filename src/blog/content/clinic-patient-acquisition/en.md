> Attracting new patients never depends on a single channel. **Google Ads** catch people who are searching for a specific treatment right now, **Meta ads** create interest among people who haven't started searching yet, and **SEO** builds a lasting presence that brings patients without paying for every click. Real success is measured in booked appointments and cost per patient, not likes.

## Why advertising alone is not enough

Many clinics launch ads, then stop a few weeks later because "ads don't work". Usually the ads are not the problem; what happens after them is. Messages go unanswered for hours, the landing page doesn't explain the treatment, or nobody follows up with the hesitant patient. That is why we treat patient acquisition as part of a full journey that starts with an ad and ends with a booking.

## Google Ads: reach patients while they search

When someone types "dental implants in Istanbul" or "best dermatology clinic near me", they are close to a decision. That is the highest buying intent you can reach.

- Choose keywords tied to the treatment and the city, not broad terms such as "doctor".
- Write ads that name the treatment and a real advantage of the clinic, such as the first consultation or the doctor's experience.
- Send visitors to a page dedicated to that treatment, not to the home page.
- Add negative keywords to exclude job seekers and people looking only for general information.

## Meta ads: create demand before the search begins

On Facebook and Instagram people aren't searching for treatment, but they respond to stories, results and educational content. What works:

- Short videos in which the doctor explains the procedure in simple words.
- Content that answers common fears: pain, cost and recovery time.
- Message campaigns that take the patient straight to WhatsApp to ask and book.

Remember that before-and-after images are subject to platform policies and, in some countries, health regulations, so review them before publishing.

## SEO: a channel that brings patients without paying per click

Search engine optimization takes time, but it builds a lasting asset for the clinic. The key steps:

1. A clear page for each service explaining the procedure, who it suits and the common questions.
2. A complete Google Business Profile with address, opening hours, photos and reviews.
3. Content that answers real patient questions, like the article you are reading.
4. A fast site that is easy to use on mobile, because most patients search from their phones.

More and more people now ask AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Gemini, so clear, well-structured content also raises the chance that your clinic is mentioned in their answers.

## How to measure success

Don't stop at clicks and impressions. The metrics that matter to a clinic owner are:

- The number of qualified inquiries from each channel.
- The rate at which inquiries become booked appointments.
- The cost of acquiring one patient.
- The show-up rate for appointments.

Connecting these numbers needs one place that holds patient data, which is where a [clinic CRM](/en/blog/clinic-crm-system) comes in.

Q: Which is better for my clinic: Google Ads or Meta ads?
A: The best results come from combining them. Google catches people searching for treatment now, while Meta builds interest and trust with a wider audience. Budget and specialty decide the split.

Q: How long does SEO take to show results?
A: Results usually start to appear within a few months and grow over time as you add useful content, improve service pages and collect genuine reviews.

Q: Do I need a website if patients already reach me on Instagram?
A: Yes. A website gives you a page for each service that can appear in Google and AI assistants, and gives patients a trusted source to check the clinic before booking.
