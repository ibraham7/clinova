> The best way to reduce no-shows is a **short sequence of WhatsApp messages**: a confirmation right after booking, a reminder the day before, and a final one on the morning of the appointment, each with an easy way to confirm or reschedule. No-shows never disappear entirely, but most patients who simply forget will come if they are reminded at the right moment.

## Why do patients miss appointments?

It is rarely a lack of interest. Patients forget, their schedule changes and they don't know how to cancel, or they feel awkward cancelling at the last minute. Every empty slot is doctor time that goes unused, and a chair another patient could have taken.

## A simple message sequence

1. **Instant confirmation after booking:** date, time, doctor's name and the clinic address.
2. **Reminder the day before:** with a clear question. Will you attend? Reply 1 to confirm or 2 to reschedule.
3. **Reminder on the morning of the visit:** a short message with the clinic's map location.
4. **Message after a no-show:** no blame, just a new slot. If it keeps happening, that patient deserves [special follow-up](/en/blog/reactivate-past-patients).

## How to write a message that gets read and answered

- **Use the name and the details:** "Hi Sara, your appointment is tomorrow, Tuesday at 4:30 pm with Dr. Ahmad."
- **Make replying easy:** one or two options, never a long paragraph.
- **Offer an alternative:** "If this time doesn't suit you, write to us and we'll find another." A patient who finds rescheduling easy doesn't disappear.
- **Use the patient's language** and a warm tone that isn't too formal.

## What to watch out for

- Don't message people who haven't agreed to be contacted, and follow the data-protection rules of your country.
- Don't send more than three messages for a single appointment.
- Review tomorrow's list at the end of the day: anyone who hasn't confirmed deserves a short call from reception.
- When a patient reschedules, fill the freed slot from your [waiting list](/en/blog/turn-inquiries-into-bookings).

## How to track the result

Record every appointment with its outcome: attended, rescheduled, cancelled or no-show. After a month, compare attendance with the number of appointments and look at which day, hour and treatment type the no-shows cluster around. These numbers are your own, so you don't need to compare them with general percentages from unknown sources. Once they sit in a [CRM](/en/blog/clinic-crm-system), the follow-up becomes automatic.

Q: How many reminders are enough for one appointment?
A: A confirmation when booking, a reminder the day before, and one on the morning of the visit. More than that annoys the patient.

Q: What should I do with a patient who doesn't reply to the reminder?
A: Reception can call them a few hours before the appointment. If they don't answer, the slot can be offered to a patient who is waiting.

Q: Do the messages have to be typed by hand?
A: No. You can use ready-made texts where the patient and appointment details change automatically, and review the wording from time to time so it stays natural.
