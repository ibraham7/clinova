> The first visit is where a patient decides whether to trust your clinic. What matters to them is not only the doctor's skill but **whether they arrived easily, were welcomed warmly, understood what would happen, and left knowing the next step**. All of it can be arranged with simple details.

## Before they arrive

- **A clear confirmation message** with the address, the map location and the nearest parking, as in [WhatsApp reminders](/en/blog/reduce-no-shows-whatsapp-reminders).
- **What to bring:** previous reports, X-rays and a list of medications, and if possible a form they fill in from their phone before arriving.
- **How long the visit will take:** many people feel tense because they don't know the duration.

## The moment of reception

- Greet them by name, introduce yourself and say what happens now.
- Don't leave them waiting without explanation. If the doctor is late, apologize and give an expected time.
- The waiting area is clean and quiet, with water and a phone charger if possible.

## During the exam

- The doctor explains what they see in simple language, without frightening terms, and shows the images if possible.
- They separate **what needs treatment now** from **what can be monitored**.
- They ask the patient what worries them and note the answer, because a patient who is heard trusts more.

## Before they leave

1. A clear plan of two or three steps, not a long list that overwhelms them.
2. A cost estimate and payment options after [explaining the plan](/en/blog/explain-treatment-plan-and-cost), without pressure.
3. The next appointment booked before they leave, if they need treatment.
4. Who will contact them, when and how.

## After the visit

A short thank-you message the same day, a summary of what the doctor said, then a follow-up after a few days if they haven't booked. This follow-up is part of the [treatment coordinator's](/en/blog/treatment-coordinator) job.

Q: What matters most in the first visit?
A: That the patient feels understood and knows the next step, because that builds trust before any treatment.

Q: Should I give a price at the first visit?
A: Yes if possible after the exam, with an explanation of what the price includes. A vague estimate loses trust.

Q: What if they leave without booking?
A: Send a thank-you message the same day and follow up gently a few days later, without pressure.
