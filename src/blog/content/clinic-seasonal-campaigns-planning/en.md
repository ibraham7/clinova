> Demand for clinics doesn't run at one pace all year: **it changes with the seasons**. Interest in cosmetic care rises before holidays and occasions, in check-ups and braces before school, and people's working hours and appointments change in Ramadan. A clinic that plans ahead benefits from the season instead of being surprised by it.

## Why plan for seasons?

- **You anticipate the pressure** and prepare the team and appointments.
- **You catch the natural interest** in a particular service at its moment.
- **You avoid empty slots** in quiet periods by encouraging check-ups.
- **You learn what works** by comparing season with season.

## Seasons you can plan for

- **Ramadan:** opening hours and suitable appointment times change for patients. Announce your hours early, and arrange appointments after iftar if possible.
- **Before holidays and occasions:** interest in cleaning and cosmetic care rises, so start preparing weeks ahead because appointments fill up.
- **Back to school:** children's check-ups and braces.
- **Summer and holidays:** many people travel, and it suits those who want to complete their treatment, as well as [medical tourism](/en/blog/medical-tourism-patient-inquiries) patients.
- **Year end:** many patients use their insurance before it expires, so remind them gently.

Choose the seasons that suit your country and your patients, since they differ from place to place.

## A simple plan for each season

1. **Six to eight weeks before:** decide what to focus on, whether to offer a promotion and what team and materials you need.
2. **Four weeks before:** prepare the messages, content, website and Google profile, as in [your clinic's Google profile](/en/blog/clinic-google-business-profile).
3. **During the season:** reply quickly and follow up inquiries daily.
4. **After the season:** compare the numbers and note what you learned.

## Promotions: with care

- Make the offer clear about what it includes and how long it lasts.
- Check that your country's laws allow the kind of offer you intend to advertise in the medical field.
- Don't let a discount harm service quality or the clinic's image.

## What to expect from inquiries

Prepare the team to reply quickly to the rise in messages and decide who follows them up, as in [replying outside working hours](/en/blog/after-hours-inquiries-auto-reply). When appointments fill up, use a waiting list and reminders to reduce no-shows, as in [WhatsApp reminders](/en/blog/reduce-no-shows-whatsapp-reminders).

## How to measure the result

Record each patient's source during the season, and compare the number of inquiries, bookings, attendance and cost per patient with the previous season, as in [marketing numbers](/en/blog/clinic-marketing-metrics-cost-per-patient).

Q: When should I start preparing for a season?
A: At least six to eight weeks ahead, because appointments fill up well before the season.

Q: Must I run a promotion every season?
A: No. Reminding patients of a service that suits the season may be enough. Check your country's laws before offering any promotion.

Q: How do I know if the season was a success?
A: Compare inquiries, bookings, attendance and cost per patient with a previous season and with what you expected.
