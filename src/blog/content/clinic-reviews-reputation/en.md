> Patients read reviews before they book. The best way to grow your clinic's reviews is to **ask every satisfied patient at the right moment** and make writing one easy, then **reply to every review** with respect. Fake or purchased reviews break Google's policies and consumer protection laws, and can lead to removed reviews or a restricted clinic listing.

## Why reviews matter for clinics

A patient chooses a doctor for their health, and sometimes their appearance, so they look for proof that others tried and succeeded. Reviews on your Google Business Profile influence that decision and the clinic's visibility in local search and Maps. AI assistants also draw on them when asked about the best clinic in a city.

## When and how to ask for a review

- **At the moment of satisfaction:** after a successful treatment, or when the patient thanks you.
- **Make it easy:** send a direct link to your review page over WhatsApp, or place a QR code at reception.
- **Ask everyone:** don't screen for happy patients first, which goes against Google's guidelines.
- **Offer nothing in return:** discounts or gifts for reviews are not allowed.

## How to reply to reviews

1. Thank positive reviewers by name and mention the service, without revealing any medical information.
2. Reply to negative reviews calmly and promptly, apologize for the experience and invite them to contact you directly to resolve it.
3. Never reveal details of a patient's case in a public reply. Medical privacy comes first.
4. Turn recurring feedback into real improvements at the clinic.

## Why you shouldn't buy reviews

Fake reviews may raise the number for a while, but they give themselves away: names that don't exist, a similar writing style, suspicious timing. Google removes them and may restrict the clinic's profile, and patients notice them and lose trust. Above all, they mislead someone making a health decision. A real reputation builds slowly, but it lasts.

## Reputation is bigger than reviews

The full patient experience is what builds a reputation: [fast replies](/en/blog/whatsapp-ai-for-clinics), clear prices, respected appointment times and [follow-up after treatment](/en/blog/treatment-coordinator). When the experience improves, positive reviews follow naturally.

Q: Can I delete negative reviews?
A: You can't delete them yourself. You can only report reviews that break Google's policies, such as offensive content or content unrelated to the clinic. A professional reply is the best answer to a genuine negative review.

Q: How many reviews does a clinic need?
A: There is no magic number, but a steady flow of recent reviews matters more than a large old batch, because patients look at review dates.

Q: Can I show Google reviews on my clinic's website?
A: Yes. You can display genuine reviews with their source, or publish patient testimonials on the site after getting their explicit consent.
