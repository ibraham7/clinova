> The **WhatsApp Business app** suits a small clinic where one or two people reply from a single phone. The **WhatsApp API** is what a clinic needs when several staff members answer on the same number, or when it wants conversations linked to patient records, appointments and follow-up. Not every clinic needs to move, but it becomes important once messages start getting lost between phones.

## What the WhatsApp Business app offers

A free phone app that adds a business profile, greeting messages, quick replies and chat labels to ordinary WhatsApp. It is easy to start and needs no technical setup.

## Where the app starts to struggle

- **The team:** the number of devices you can link to one number is limited, so staff share a phone or compete to reply.
- **No context:** a staff member opening a chat can't tell which appointment the patient booked or what a colleague said yesterday without reading the whole thread.
- **Limited automation:** quick replies alone aren't enough for appointment reminders and follow-ups.
- **Manual follow-up:** no tasks and no stages, so everything depends on someone's memory. That is what causes [lost inquiries](/en/blog/clinic-crm-system).

## What the API adds

- **Several staff on one number:** each person signs in with their own account and permissions, and a conversation can be handed from one person to another.
- **Integration with other systems:** the conversation appears inside the patient's record with their appointments and stage, and a message can become a task.
- **Templates for messages the clinic starts:** appointment reminders and follow-ups are usually sent with approved templates, and the platform has rules about the window for replying to a patient.
- **Monitoring:** who replied and when, and how many conversations are waiting for an answer.

## When to move

Start thinking about moving if two of these apply to you:

1. More than one person answers patients.
2. Replies are delayed or "I didn't see the message" keeps coming up.
3. You want automatic [appointment reminders](/en/blog/reduce-no-shows-whatsapp-reminders) and follow-ups.
4. You want to know which ad campaign brought which patient.

## Before you move

- **Check the platform's policies and terms:** they change from time to time, and cost and setup differ by service provider.
- **Don't lose your conversations:** ask your provider what happens to the number and the existing chat history before connecting.
- **Tell your team about the change:** a new system doesn't help if some people keep replying from their personal phones.

Q: Do I need the API if my clinic has only one receptionist?
A: Probably not. If one person replies and the message volume is low, the regular app is enough. Think about moving when the team or the volume grows.

Q: Do I lose my current number when I move?
A: It depends on how you connect and on your service provider, so ask before you start and keep a copy of important conversations.

Q: Does the API mean bulk messaging?
A: No. Messages the clinic starts follow rules set by the platform, and too many promotional messages can get a number restricted. Use it for useful reminders and follow-ups.
