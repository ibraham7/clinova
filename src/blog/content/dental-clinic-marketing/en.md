> Successful dental clinic marketing starts with the **service**, not the ad. Pick the highest-value treatments, such as implants, orthodontics and smile makeovers, and build a page, content and a campaign for each one. Then connect it all to a team that replies fast and follows up until the booking. If you treat patients from abroad, add a clear experience covering coordination, accommodation and follow-up after they return home.

## 1. Start with the services that make the difference

Not every service is equal in marketing. Implants, orthodontics and veneers involve a bigger decision and bring higher value, while fillings and cleanings bring repeat patients. Choose two or three services to focus on and build everything around them.

## 2. A website that answers before patients ask

Give each service a page that explains who it suits, how it's done, how long it takes and what to expect afterwards, with common questions and real photos from the clinic. These pages are what appear in Google and what AI assistants read when answering patients' questions. [Read the guide to attracting patients with Google, Meta and SEO](/en/blog/clinic-patient-acquisition).

## 3. Content that builds trust

- Short videos in which the doctor explains the procedure and answers concerns.
- A tour of the clinic, its equipment and sterilization.
- Real patient stories shared with consent, respecting platform rules on before-and-after images.
- [Genuine Google reviews](/en/blog/clinic-reviews-reputation) that the clinic team replies to.

## 4. Focused campaigns for each service

Run a Google campaign for people searching "dental implants" in your city, and a Meta campaign that tells a success story and opens a WhatsApp chat. Don't mix all services in one campaign: each treatment has its own audience and questions.

## 5. Replies and follow-up create the booking

Most inquiries start with a question about price. [Qualify the patient, answer their concerns and follow up](/en/blog/turn-inquiries-into-bookings), and bring in a [treatment coordinator](/en/blog/treatment-coordinator) for big cases such as full-arch implants.

## 6. Dental tourism patients

If your clinic welcomes patients from other countries, they need extra information: length of stay, number of visits, what the price includes and follow-up after they return. Offer contact in their language, a written treatment plan before they travel, and a coordinator from arrival to departure.
