> A patient who sends a question at 11 pm may book with another clinic that answers faster. **AI replies on WhatsApp** answer frequent questions instantly, collect the patient's details and suggest an appointment, while the **human team** handles sensitive cases and treatment decisions during working hours.

## Why response speed matters

A patient looking for treatment often contacts several clinics at once. The first clinic to reply clearly and respectfully earns their trust. Delays of several hours, especially in the evening and at weekends, mean part of your inquiries go to competitors.

## What can AI do?

- Greet the patient in their language and answer common questions: services, opening hours, location and how to book.
- Collect the basics: name, treatment of interest and preferred time.
- Suggest available slots or pass the request to the team to confirm.
- Send a reminder before the appointment to reduce no-shows.
- Log the conversation in the [CRM](/en/blog/clinic-crm-system) so nothing gets lost.

## What must stay with people?

AI does not diagnose or prescribe. It should hand the conversation to the team immediately when:

1. The patient asks about a specific medical condition or symptoms.
2. The patient is anxious or upset, or needs reassurance.
3. The patient asks to speak to a real person.
4. The question concerns a treatment plan or a final price that needs an assessment.

This balance is what we call **AI + Human**: the machine's speed in replying, and the team's humanity in deciding.

## How to start the right way

1. Write the twenty most frequent questions with answers approved by the clinic.
2. Define exactly when automated replies stop and the team steps in.
3. Tell patients clearly that they are talking to an automated assistant. Transparency builds trust.
4. Use the official WhatsApp Business API, and follow Meta's policies and the patient's consent for messages.
5. Review conversations every week and improve the answers.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Long, rigid replies that don't sound like your clinic.
- Pushing for a booking before answering the patient's question.
- No human follow-up for conversations that stalled.
- Repeated promotional messages without permission.

Q: Do automated replies annoy patients?
A: Only bad ones do. A fast, clear reply that hands the patient to a person when needed improves their experience.

Q: Can AI reply in Arabic and in different dialects?
A: Yes. Modern models understand standard Arabic and common dialects, but the approved answers should still be reviewed in your clinic's voice.

Q: Can the AI assistant connect to the clinic's appointments?
A: Yes. It can connect to the booking system or CRM to suggest available slots and log requests directly.
