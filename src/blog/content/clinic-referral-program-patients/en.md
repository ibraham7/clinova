> A referral from a satisfied patient is the strongest form of marketing for a clinic, because people trust those they know more than any advertisement. But referrals rarely come on their own: **a satisfied patient recommends when asked kindly and when it is easy**. A simple program is enough.

## Why do referrals work?

- The referred patient arrives already trusting the clinic, so there is less hesitation.
- The cost of attracting them is low compared with ads.
- They often stay loyal and recommend in turn.

## When to ask for a referral

The best time is **after a good experience**: at the end of a successful treatment, or when the patient expresses satisfaction. Don't ask on the first visit, or before they are reassured about the result. See [post-treatment follow-up messages](/en/blog/post-treatment-follow-up-messages).

## How to ask

- A short, sincere message: "We're glad to have served you. If you have a friend or relative who needs dental care, we'd be happy to look after them."
- **Make sharing easy:** a link or card the patient can send with one tap.
- Don't pressure them or make them feel obliged.

## How to thank someone who recommends you

- **A sincere thank-you first:** a personal thank-you message.
- A symbolic gift or small gesture if you wish, **after checking that your country's laws and medical ethics allow it**, because some countries restrict incentives in medical promotion.
- Don't make the incentive so large that it encourages insincere recommendations.

## What to avoid

- Don't ask a patient to post a review in exchange for a discount: it breaks platform policies and may break the law.
- Don't reveal who referred someone without their permission.
- Don't use photos or stories of patients without their explicit consent.
- Don't treat the referred patient any differently from others, and don't forget service quality.

## How to track the result

Record "referral from so-and-so" as the new patient's source, and see how many patients came this way and how many of them booked and attended, as in [marketing numbers](/en/blog/clinic-marketing-metrics-cost-per-patient).

## Google reviews are part of the referral

A public recommendation on Google complements a personal one. Ask for the review kindly, as in [your clinic's Google profile](/en/blog/clinic-google-business-profile) and [reviews and reputation](/en/blog/clinic-reviews-reputation).

Q: Should I give a patient a reward for a referral?
A: First ask what your country's laws and medical ethics allow. A sincere thank-you is always fine, while material incentives may be restricted.

Q: What is the best time to ask for a referral?
A: After a good experience, such as the end of a successful treatment or when the patient expresses satisfaction.

Q: Do I need a formal program?
A: No. It is enough to ask kindly, make it easy and record the source of new patients.
