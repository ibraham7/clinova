> Every clinic has an untapped asset: patients who asked but never booked, patients who started treatment and didn't finish, and patients who never came back for their check-up. **Reactivation campaigns** reach each of these groups with a fitting message, and they usually cost far less than winning a brand-new patient because these people already know the clinic.

## Which patients can be reactivated?

- **Inquiries that never booked:** they asked about a treatment, then got busy.
- **Unfinished treatments:** patients who stopped halfway through a plan.
- **Overdue check-ups:** patients due for their routine examination or cleaning.
- **Satisfied past patients:** they may need another service or recommend friends.

## Steps for a successful reactivation campaign

1. **Clean the data:** gather names and numbers from notebooks, files and chats into a [CRM](/en/blog/clinic-crm-system) and remove duplicates.
2. **Segment patients:** each group needs a different message. Someone who didn't finish treatment needs a different approach from someone who only asked.
3. **Write a personal message:** use the patient's name and the treatment they asked about, and start by checking on them rather than with an offer.
4. **Give a reason to come back:** a free review consultation, an available slot soon, or a time-limited offer with clear terms.
5. **Answer replies fast:** every reply is a [new inquiry to turn into a booking](/en/blog/turn-inquiries-into-bookings).

## Respect privacy and consent

Only contact patients who agreed to receive messages from the clinic, and follow your country's data protection laws and WhatsApp's rules on marketing messages. Always include an easy way to opt out, and honor it immediately. An annoying campaign can cost you more in reputation than it brings in bookings.

## How to measure the campaign

Track the reply rate, booked appointments, show-up rate, and the revenue generated compared with the campaign's cost. Run the campaign regularly rather than once.

Q: How big does the database need to be for a campaign to work?
A: Even a few hundred past patients can be enough for a useful campaign, because the contact is personal and the cost is low.

Q: Is WhatsApp or a phone call better?
A: WhatsApp is a good start because it is less intrusive. Calls help with patients who showed interest or stopped halfway through treatment.

Q: Can patients whose last visit was years ago be reactivated?
A: Yes, as long as you have their consent to be contacted and the message is relevant, respectful and gives them a clear reason to return.
