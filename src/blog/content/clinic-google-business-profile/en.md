> When a patient searches for "dentist near me", the first thing they see is the **Google Business Profile** and a map of clinics. A complete, accurate and up-to-date profile gives your clinic a chance to be seen and called directly, and it is one of the cheapest and most useful ways to attract local patients.

## What is a Business Profile?

It is your clinic's card in Google Search and Maps: name, address, phone, opening hours, photos and reviews. Creating it is free.

## The basic steps

1. **Create the profile and verify ownership** following the steps Google asks for (often a postcard, a call or a video).
2. **Choose the right category:** such as "dental clinic", and add secondary categories for the services you offer.
3. **Enter the name, address and phone accurately** and in the same format as on your website and everywhere else.
4. **Add opening hours and keep them updated** for holidays and special occasions.
5. **Link your website** and a booking link or WhatsApp.
6. **Add services** with a short description of each.
7. **Add photos:** the clinic's front, reception, rooms and team, using real pictures.

## Reviews

- Ask satisfied patients for a review after treatment, with a kind message and a direct link. See [reviews and reputation](/en/blog/clinic-reviews-reputation).
- Reply to every review, positive or negative, politely. Replying to a negative one shows professionalism.
- Don't buy reviews or write them yourself: it breaks Google's policies and can get the profile suspended.
- Don't mention a patient's health details in your reply.

## What helps you appear?

- **Complete and accurate information** that matches your website.
- **Proximity to the searcher** and relevance of the service to what they are looking for.
- **Reviews and activity:** updated photos and regular posts and offers.
- **Real photos** instead of stock ones.

Nobody can guarantee a particular position, but these basics are what you control.

## Messages that arrive from the profile

Many patients call or write to you directly from the profile. Make sure replies are fast and that messages reach a place the team sees, as in [reply speed](/en/blog/turn-inquiries-into-bookings).

Q: Is creating the profile free?
A: Yes, creating it is free, and it requires verifying that you own the clinic.

Q: How often should I update the profile?
A: Review it at least monthly, and update it immediately when the address, phone or opening hours change.

Q: Can I reward patients for a review?
A: Don't offer a reward in exchange for a review, because Google's policies forbid it. Ask kindly without promises.
